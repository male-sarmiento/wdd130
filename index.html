About Me

Hello! My name is Maria Sarmiento, and I am from Santander, Colombia. I love attending church because it is an important part of my life. I also enjoy spending quality time with my family and going on trips together.

My Hobbies and Favorite Memories

My family and I love visiting the beautiful beaches in Colombia. One of my most unforgettable vacations was our trip to the San Bernardo Archipelago. During that experience, I had the amazing opportunity to swim with sharks and enjoy the beautiful Caribbean waters. It was an exciting and unforgettable adventure!

Colombia

Colombia is a breathtaking country in South America, and one of its Caribbean treasures is the San Bernardo Archipelago. It is famous for its dazzling turquoise waters, vibrant coral reefs, and peaceful white-sand islands like Isla Múcura and Tintipán. Visitors can enjoy snorkeling, see luminous plankton at night, and explore Santa Cruz del Islote, one of the most densely populated islands in the world.
