White Water Rafting

We are a family-run rafting company that guides small groups down some of the most exciting rivers in the region. Our certified guides focus on safety first, so beginners and experienced paddlers can enjoy the rapids, the canyon views, and the time together on the water.

History

Our company started more than twenty years ago with one raft, two guides, and a love for the river. Over the years we trained new guides, added new boats, and opened routes for families and first-time paddlers.

Today we offer half-day, full-day, and multi-day trips. We still follow the same values we had on day one: respect for the river, safety on every trip, and memories that last a lifetime.
